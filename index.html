About Me

I specialize in real-time rendering, GPU programming, and advanced computer vision systems. With over 20 years of experience, I design and build high-performance solutions across 3D visual computing systems, image processing, and modern deep learning approaches.

I have worked across a wide range of problems, from image analysis and multiview geometry to photogrammetry and real-time physically-based rendering. My focus is on turning complex mathematical models into efficient, production-ready systems, with particular expertise in GPU optimization, performance-critical applications, and scalable solutions. Beyond hands-on development, I bring experience in leading projects, mentoring teams, and delivering results in demanding, fast-paced environments.

Recently, I have been focusing on ray tracing, diffusion-based models, spatial computing and radiance fields.
