About Me

My name is Umut Kocak, and I am a seasoned professional in computer graphics and computer vision, with a career that began in 2004. My journey, which started with image processing, has led me through a diverse array of projects, including registration, segmentation, volume rendering, haptic rendering, physics engines, real-time physically-based rendering, multiview geometry, photogrammetry, 3D reconstruction, structure from motion(sfm), ray tracing, and deep learning.

I find joy in understanding the intricate relationships between mathematical models and real-world phenomena, especially those involving visual imagination. I have mostly worked on demanding real-time interactive projects, and as a result, I have developed expertise in GPU programming, code optimization, and memory monitoring. Having worked extensively with classical graphical rendering and computer vision algorithms, I have also recently developed a strong interest in modern deep learning techniques, among which I am particularly interested in diffusion models.

Apart from technical challenges, I have occasionally taken on diverse roles, including teaching, mentorship, project management, Scrum Master, and team lead.

I prefer to spend my free time actively outdoors. In the past, I’ve enjoyed snowboarding, saiing, playing tennis, and running, but lately, switched to more sustainable activities like freestyle swimming. My lifelong passion also includes partner dances such as salsa, bachata, and zouk.

After living in different countries, I eventually settled in Berlin, where I started my own family and discovered my latest passion—spending time outdoors with my son.💗
